# Data provenance

**QCAR628BIS — Real Residential Property Prices for Canada.** Quarterly, not seasonally adjusted; index 2010 = 100. Source: Bank for International Settlements, distributed through FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.

Official series: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/QCAR628BIS

The checked-in CSV is the unchanged snapshot supplied with the academic project: 222 observations, 1970-01-01 to 2025-04-01. Its original retrieval date is unknown. The upstream series can be revised; a new download will not necessarily reproduce historical figures.

Required source credit: “Sources: National sources, BIS Residential Property Price database, http://www.bis.org/statistics/pp.htm.” BIS terms: https://www.bis.org/terms_conditions.htm#Copyright_and_Permissions . These source terms are separate from the repository’s code.
